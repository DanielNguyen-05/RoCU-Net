RoCU-Net recovers fine polyp boundaries by redistributing coarse foreground occupancy to finer resolutions, with exact parent–child consistency and only 0.28M parameters.

Abstract

Polyp segmentation from colonoscopy images is crucial for computer-aided colorectal cancer detection, yet it faces challenges due to diverse polyp appearances and the strict computational budgets of real-time clinical deployment. Existing lightweight models often struggle to balance efficiency with precision, typically losing fine boundary details during upsampling. To address this, we propose RoCU-Net, a highly efficient encoder-decoder architecture driven by a novel Routed Occupancy-Constrained Upsampling (RoCU) mechanism. Rather than relying on computationally heavy refinement modules, RoCU intelligently distributes coarse occupancy probabilities to higher-resolution pixels. This allocation is guided by a compact semantic carrier and regulated by an uncertainty-modulated gate, strictly preserving the mathematical consistency between resolution scales. To further optimize inference, we introduce a routing strategy that allows high-confidence regions to bypass the iterative allocation solver, significantly reducing computational overhead. Extensive evaluations on the CVC-ClinicDB, CVC-ColonDB, and Kvasir-SEG datasets demonstrate that RoCU-Net matches or outperforms recent state-of-the-art lightweight baselines. Operating with merely 0.3M parameters and 1.1 GFLOPs, our model achieves highly competitive Dice and IoU scores across all benchmarks. Comprehensive ablation studies confirm that our occupancy constraints, propagated carrier, and adaptive blending effectively synergize to deliver sharp, precise boundaries under stringent resource constraints.

Method

A lightweight decoder has the least capacity to spare exactly where boundary detail is recovered: the final upsampling steps. RoCU-Net asks how a compact decoder should turn a coarse foreground estimate into finer predictions. Each coarse cell is read as a local foreground occupancy, and its four children at the next resolution are required to keep the same mean probability. This separates how much foreground a cell contains from where inside the cell it lies, and makes cross-scale consistency directly verifiable.

1

Occupancy-constrained allocation

For each parent cell with occupancy \(p\), one shared offset \(b\) turns four child scores into bounded occupancies whose mean matches the parent:

\[ r_j = \sigma(s_j + b), \qquad \tfrac{1}{4}\textstyle\sum_{j=1}^{4} r_j = p. \]

Because the sigmoid is monotone, the scores decide the spatial distribution while \(b\) alone fixes the total. \(b\) is found by 24 bisection steps; gradients follow from the implicit function theorem at the root.

2

Propagated semantic carrier

A scalar probability says nothing about where the contour lies inside a cell. A 32-channel carrier \(Z\) travels with the occupancy through both high-resolution transitions:

\[ Z^{+} = \Phi\big(\mathrm{PS}_2(A_Z(Z)) + A_E(E)\big). \]

It drives a boundary head, a gate head and the score head, using encoder features at 1/2 resolution and a 24-channel full-resolution shallow guide.

3

Shared-gate blending and routing

An ambiguity-modulated gate, shared by the four children, blends the refined allocation with exact parent replication:

\[ u = 4p(1-p), \quad g = u\,\hat g, \quad q_j = (1-g)\,p + g\,r_j. \]

The mean is preserved for any \(g\). At inference, cells with \(u < \tau_r\) skip the solver; the change in each child is bounded by about 0.0077 at \(\tau_r = 0.10\).

Related links

LV-UNet pairs a pretrained MobileNetV3-Large encoder with re-parameterizable modules; it is the strongest lightweight baseline in our comparison, and its benchmark provides the baseline results above.

PraNet refines polyp predictions with reverse attention driven by coarse region and boundary cues. RoCU-Net also reuses intermediate predictions, but gives the coarse prediction an explicit numerical role.

CARAFE and DySample learn content-adaptive feature upsampling. We insert both into the RoCU-Net scaffold as upsampling-operator controls.

Hard-Constrained Deep Learning for Climate Downscaling enforces conservation between coarse and fine physical fields through the output layer, a precedent for conservation-constrained resolution recovery.
